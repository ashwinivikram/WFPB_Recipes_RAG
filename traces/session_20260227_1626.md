**Rich Chocolate Cake with Chocolate Frosting and Strawberries**
Creator: Kumar Natarajan Satvic

**Cake Ingredients:**
*   1 cup oat flour
*   1/2 cup almond flour
*   1/2 cup cacao powder
*   2 tsp baking powder
*   1.5 cups deglet dates
*   1 tsp vanilla powder or extract
*   1 tsp ACV (apple cider vinegar)
*   2 cups unsweetened soy milk
*   Several strawberries

**Frosting Ingredients:**
*   4-5 deglet dates
*   1 tbsp almond flour
*   1 tbsp cacao powder
*   1/2 tsp vanilla powder or extract
*   1/2 cup hot water

**Method:**
1.  **Dry mixture:** Mix oat flour, almond flour, cacao powder, baking powder, and vanilla powder in a bowl.
2.  **Wet mixture:** Blend dates, ACV, and 1 cup soy milk until smooth.
3.  **Combine:** Pour wet mixture into dry, add another 1 cup soy milk, mix well.
4.  **Bake:** Bake in a small round pan for 20 min at 350 F.
5.  **Frosting:** Soak dates in hot water, then blend with almond flour, cacao powder, and vanilla powder until very smooth.
6.  **Assemble:** Pour frosting over cooled cake, even it out, and top with inverted strawberries. Apple sauce could be added for more moisture.
